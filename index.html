About 5–8 minutes · No account, API key, or internet connection required

Before you begin

Choose the option that feels more natural to you most of the time—not what you think is best, what a teacher expects, or what you can do when required. There are no right or wrong answers. If both seem true, choose the one you tend to prefer when you are relaxed.

Important: This is an educational self-reflection activity inspired by the four MBTI preference dimensions. It is not the official MBTI assessment, a clinical or psychological diagnosis, or a scientifically validated personality test. Results are indicative prompts for reflection, not fixed labels. Your preferences can vary by context and change over time.
